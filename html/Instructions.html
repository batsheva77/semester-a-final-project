🚀 Mission: Asteroid Navigation

Welcome, Captain! Your mission is to guide your spaceship safely without hitting unstable asteroids that will cause a ship explosion. Here are your mission protocols:

  1. Scanning the Area: Click a tile to scan for danger. The number revealed tells you how many asteroids are hidden in the 8 surrounding tiles.
  2. Avoid Explosion: If you click a tile containing an asteroid (☄️), your ship will be destroyed and the mission ends.
  3. Marking Danger: Use the Flag (Right-Click) to mark tiles where you suspect an asteroid is hidden to avoid accidental contact.
  4. Victory: The mission is successful once all safe space sectors are revealed.
  5. Abort Mission: Click the "LOG OUT" button to return to base.
